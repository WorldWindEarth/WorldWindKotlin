iOS port of TiledElevationCoverage. Two retrieval paths:

  • TileSourceElevationRef — cache-first reads through the earth.worldwind.layer.cache.CachedTileSource decorator. Output format comes from the ref, no Content-Type negotiation needed.

  • URL — direct Ktor GET, dispatched by Content-Type header.

Both paths share decodeBytes which understands application/bil16, application/bil32, image/tiff (GeoTIFF), and application/dted{,0,1,2}. PNG-encoded elevation tiles fall through with a WARN — add a decoder if any consumer wires that format.
