class ElevationImage(val array: ShortArray)

A decoded elevation tile (ShortArray, NW origin) plus min/max/missing stats computed once at construction, so height-limit queries that fully contain the tile skip the per-texel rescan. Missing posts are Short.MIN_VALUE and excluded from the min/max.
