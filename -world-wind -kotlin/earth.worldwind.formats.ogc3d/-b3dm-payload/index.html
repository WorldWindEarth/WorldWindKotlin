B3dmPayload

class B3dmPayload

Decoded b3dm payload: the embedded glTF + feature table (for RTC_CENTER + optional gltfUpAxis override) + the batch table. The renderer applies rtcCenter as an extra translation when present — per spec, b3dm authors may offset every vertex by a tile- relative center to keep numerical precision tight at city scale.
