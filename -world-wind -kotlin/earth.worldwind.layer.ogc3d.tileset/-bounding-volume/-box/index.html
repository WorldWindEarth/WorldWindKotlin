Oriented box: ECEF center + 3 half-axis vectors. Layout per spec is [cx, cy, cz, hX.x, hX.y, hX.z, hY.x, hY.y, hY.z, hZ.x, hZ.y, hZ.z]. Coordinates are in tile-local frame; the tile-to-world transform maps them into ECEF.
