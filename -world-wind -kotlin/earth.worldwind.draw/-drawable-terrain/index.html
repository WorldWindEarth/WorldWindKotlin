boundingSphereRadius

open val boundingSphereRadius: Double

World-space bounding sphere radius around vertexOrigin that conservatively contains every vertex of this terrain tile (including peak elevation). Used by per-cascade cullers (see earth.worldwind.layer.shadow.ShadowState.CascadeState.intersectsSphere) to skip tiles that fall entirely outside a cascade's footprint. 0 opts out of culling.
