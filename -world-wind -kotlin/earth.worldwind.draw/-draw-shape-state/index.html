val boundingCenter: Vec3

World-space bounding sphere of this shape, used by the shadow cascade dispatcher to skip cascades whose AABB doesn't intersect the shape. boundingRadius left at 0.0 opts out of culling (the shape is rasterised into every cascade). Populated by shapes whose currentBoundindData.boundingBox is meaningful.
