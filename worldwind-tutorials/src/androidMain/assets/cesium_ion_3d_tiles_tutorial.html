Cesium Ion 3D Tiles Tutorial

Streams a Cesium Ion-hosted 3D Tiles asset. CesiumIonAuthProvider attaches the user's Ion access token, follows Ion's assets/<id>/endpoint redirect to the real tileset URL, and reuses the short-lived per-asset access token returned by that redirect on every subsequent tile fetch.

Paste your Cesium Ion access token into CesiumIon3dTilesTutorial.cesiumIonAccessToken (generate one at ion.cesium.com/tokens). Override cesiumIonAssetId and cameraLookAt to swap to any other Ion asset. The default is Aerometrex Melbourne photogrammetry — a free public asset in the Ion Asset Depot.
