3D Tiles Archive Tutorial

Renders a local Cesium 3D Tiles Archive — a single-file package of an ordinary 3D Tiles dataset. Both container forms are supported and auto-detected by magic bytes: .3tz (a ZIP) and .3dtiles (a SQLite media(key, content) database).

openArchived3dTilesLayer(file) wires the archive in purely as the layer's byte transport — nothing is copied or extracted, tile bytes are read straight from the archive at render time, and the standard parse/decode/traverse/GPU pipeline runs unchanged. Call Ogc3dTilesLayer.shutdown() when done to close the archive handle.

The tutorial stages the bundled dragon sample archive into the app cache dir on first use (the archive readers only open real filesystem files), then frames the camera on the dataset extent once tileset.json parses.
